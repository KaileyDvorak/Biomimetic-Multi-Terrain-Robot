# Biomimetic Multi-Terrain Robot

An ongoing independent research project exploring a biologically
inspired robotic platform capable of terrestrial, aerial, and aquatic
locomotion.

## Motivation

I believe that such a versatile machine can help so many people in various different fields. There's a lot of advancements in robotics that are incredibly impressive, however, I feel that we can take a step further. With this design, our machines won't be limited to only being on the ground or only being in the sky. Now we'll be able to explore land, water, and air and hopefully we can go beyond that.

## Biological Inspiration

- Spider morphology
- Insect locomotion
- Multi-appendage structures
- Environmental adaptation
